Why the areas are true

A Voronoi diagram over scattered seeds looks like this but means nothing — the cell sizes are an accident of where the seeds landed. This is a weighted power diagram, solved until every room matches its share. The figure above the plan is the largest error still present; press Re-survey and the rooms rearrange while the areas hold.

Why area is not enough

People read area to about ±20%, so area alone would be a soft claim. Every room is therefore direct-labelled with its exact count, and the table below carries the same figures as numbers — the shape gives you the gestalt, the label gives you the value.

Why this colour scale

Voice-versus-taste is a polarity, not an identity, so it takes a diverging ramp — two poles and a neutral middle — rather than one colour per room. Both poles were checked against this surface for contrast and for red–green colour blindness, in both themes.
